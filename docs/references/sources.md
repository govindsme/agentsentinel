## Notes on citation and authority

- **Authority ratings:** `lawful` (binding law), `standard` (consensus standard),
  `high` (authoritative guidance), `medium` (industry guidance).
- **Sourced fact vs. inference:** content distinguishes sourced facts from technical
  observation and recommendation; see [Claims](../research/research-state.md) for claim
  traceability.
- **Legal precision:** this site is a reference, not legal advice; verify obligations
  with qualified counsel.
