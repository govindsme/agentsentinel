# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

The **NIST AI RMF** is a voluntary framework published in January 2023 to help
organisations manage AI risk and promote **trustworthy and responsible** AI development
and use. A **Generative AI Profile** (July 2024) applies it to generative AI risks.

## The four functions

The framework is organized around the AI lifecycle:

1. **Govern** — cultivate a risk-management culture; establish governance, policy, and accountability.
2. **Map** — understand the AI context, risks, and the AI system's functionality and beneficiaries.
3. **Measure** — evaluate, analyze, and track AI risks and trustworthy characteristics.
4. **Manage** — treat and prioritize risks, integrate with organizational processes.

These functions are applied cyclically and iteratively across the AI lifecycle
(plan, design, develop, deploy, operate, monitor).

## Trustworthy AI characteristics

NIST relates risk to managing these characteristics:

- **Valid & reliable** · **Safe** · **Secure & resilient** · **Accountable & transparent**
- **Explainable & interpretable** · **Privacy-enhanced** · **Fair with harmful-bias managed**

## The Generative AI Profile

The Generative AI Profile identifies the unique risks of generative AI and maps
**mitigations** to AI RMF actions, including:

- Confabulation / hallucination
- Data privacy risks and malicious use
- Toxicity, bias, and harmful content
- Security risks (prompt injection, data poisoning)
- Supply-chain and model-theft risks
