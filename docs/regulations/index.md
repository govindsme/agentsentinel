# Regulatory Landscape

AI governance is increasingly shaped by **law and regulation** across jurisdictions.
This section tracks the instruments that most affect organisations building, deploying,
or procuring AI.

!!! warning "Fast-moving landscape"
    AI law and guidance evolve quickly. **Revalidate instrument status and applicability
    on every site run.** Nothing here is legal advice.

## Primary instruments

| Instrument | Jurisdiction | Status | Applies to |
|------------|--------------|--------|------------|
| [EU AI Act](eu-ai-act.md) | EU | In force (Aug 2024) | AI systems placed on / used in the EU |
| [NIST AI RMF](nist-ai-rmf.md) | US (voluntary) | Adopted | Organizations managing AI risk |
| [ISO/IEC 42001](iso-42001.md) | International | Published 2023 | Organizations seeking AIMS certification |
| GDPR | EU/EEA | In force (2018) | Processing of EU personal data |

## Route by reader

- **Building for the EU market** → [EU AI Act](eu-ai-act.md) + GDPR.
- **Establishing voluntary risk management** → [NIST AI RMF](nist-ai-rmf.md).
- **Pursuing certification** → [ISO/IEC 42001](iso-42001.md).
- **Operating globally** → [Jurisdiction Matrix](jurisdiction-matrix.md).

Track emerging and evolving instruments across jurisdictions (UK, Canada, China, Singapore,

## Approach

For each instrument, this site documents:

- **Scope** — what it applies to.
- **Obligations** — what organisations must do.
- **Timeline** — key dates and deadlines.
- **Controls** — how to implement and verify compliance.
