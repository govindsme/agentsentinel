# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 - AI Management System

**ISO/IEC 42001:2023** is the first international, **certifiable** AI management system
(AIMS) standard. It provides a framework for governing AI within the context of an
organisation's management system, aligned with the high-level structure of other ISO
management standards (e.g., ISO/IEC 27001).

## What the standard covers

- **Context of the organisation** — understanding AI-related issues and stakeholder expectations.
- **Leadership** — AI policy, roles, and responsibility.
- **Planning** — AI risk assessment and risk treatment (Annex B provides an AI risk reference).
- **Support** — resources, competence, and awareness for AI.
- **Operation** — operational controls and AI risk treatment.
- **Performance evaluation** — monitoring, measurement, audit, and management review.
- **Improvement** — continual improvement of the AI management system.

## AI-specific annexes

- **Annex A** — Controls reference for the AI management system (objectives and controls).
- **Annex B** — AI risk sources and risk treatment considerations.
- **Annex C** — Organisational objectives and risk sources in the use of AI systems.

## How it relates to other standards

- **ISO/IEC 23894:2023** — guidance on **AI risk management**, aligned with ISO 31000.
- **ISO/IEC 42005** (consultation) — AI system impact assessment.
- **ISO/IEC 27001** — information security; AIMS complements it where AI processes information.
