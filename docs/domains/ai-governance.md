# AI Governance & Responsible AI

AI governance is the system of **policies, roles, processes, and controls** that ensures
AI is developed and used in a way that is lawful, ethical, robust, and aligned with
organisational objectives and societal expectations.

## Governance pillars

1. **Accountability** — clear ownership for each AI system and its risks.
2. **Policy** — AI policy, acceptable-use, and ethical principles.
3. **Oversight** — governance bodies (e.g., AI ethics / risk committees).
4. **Lifecycle governance** — govern from design through deployment and monitoring.
5. **Transparency** — document purpose, data, and limitations.
6. **Auditability** — evidence trail supporting internal and external assurance.

## Building an AI governance program

### 1. Establish the AI inventory
The foundation of governance. See the [AI Inventory Template](../resources/ai-inventory.md).
Maintain a catalog of every AI system, model, and use case with owners and risk ratings.

### 2. Define roles and responsibilities
- **Executives / Board** — AI strategy, risk appetite, accountability.
- **CISO** — security of AI systems and AI-enabled security.
- **GRC** — policy, framework mapping, audit.
- **Data / Privacy** — data governance and privacy impact.
- **Engineering / MLOps** — secure build and operation.
- **Procurement / Legal** — supplier risk and contracts.

### 3. Adopt a governance framework
Align to established frameworks to accelerate maturity:

- **NIST AI RMF** (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage) — risk-based practices.
- **ISO/IEC 42001** — certifiable AI management system (AIMS).
- **EU AI Act** — binding obligations for EU-placed systems.

### 4. Enforce policy through controls
Translate policy into enforceable controls mapped to frameworks.
See [Controls & Frameworks](../controls/index.md) and [Control Crosswalks](../controls/crosswalks.md).

### 5. Demonstrate auditability
- Keep records of decisions, risk assessments, and controls operation.
- Maintain traceability from policy → risk → control → evidence.
- Support **conformity assessment** where regulation requires it (e.g., EU AI Act high-risk).

## Responsible AI trust attributes

Assess and govern these trust characteristics across the lifecycle:

- **Valid & reliable** · **Safe** · **Secure & resilient** · **Accountable & transparent**
- **Explainable & interpretable** · **Privacy-enhanced** · **Fair with harmful-bias managed**
